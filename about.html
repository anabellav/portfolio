About

Hola, I’m Anabella 👋

I’m Anabella, a Senior Product Designer based in Hitchin.

I’ve worked in UX and product design for more than 10 years across mortgages, banking, publishing and B2B software. I enjoy taking complicated products and making them clearer and easier to use.

I studied art and like keeping a creative side outside work, exploring less structured environments through pottery, crochet and woodwork.

I currently volunteer with Letchworth Garden City Heritage Foundation. These days I’m also increasingly interested in culture, community and the role creative spaces can play in bringing people together.
